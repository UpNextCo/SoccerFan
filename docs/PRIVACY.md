# Ball Knowledge — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** June 16, 2026

Ball Knowledge ("we", "our", "the app") is a daily football puzzle app.

## Information we collect

- **Apple Sign In:** We receive a unique Apple user identifier and, if you choose to share it, your display name.
- **Game progress:** XP, streak, daily completion scores, and gameplay statistics.
- **Device data:** Standard app diagnostics for crash reporting (if enabled).

We do not sell your personal information.

## How we use information

- To authenticate your account and sync progress across devices
- To operate daily challenges and leaderboards
- To improve the app experience

## Data retention

You may delete your account at any time from the Profile tab. This removes your account and associated progress from our servers.

## Third parties

- **Apple** — authentication (Sign in with Apple)
- **Railway** — backend hosting
- **API-Football** — football statistics (server-side only; no direct client access)

## Contact

For privacy questions: privacy@ballknowledge.app

## Changes

We may update this policy. Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
